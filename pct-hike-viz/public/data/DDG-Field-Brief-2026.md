## Emergency coordination

This is a coordination reference, not medical treatment instruction. In an emergency, use the fastest available emergency channel.

1. **Immediate threat to life or property** — Call 911 when cellular service is available. Without service, activate the tested satellite SOS device and keep the group together unless staying creates a greater immediate danger.
2. **Send the facts that rescuers need** — Send the exact coordinate, PCT and route mile, condition, injuries or hazards, party count, intended movement, and battery / communication status. Do not replace facts with a vague 'help' message.
3. **Preserve navigation and communication capability** — Keep one phone and the satellite device powered, protect them from water and cold, and write down the last confirmed location if screens or batteries fail.
4. **Notify the preassigned contact only after emergency contact is underway** — Use the actual team contact tree and check-in protocol after the emergency channel is engaged. The app deliberately does not invent a family contact or a rescue ETA.
